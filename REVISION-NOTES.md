# Notes for the next revision pass

These are changes in the KAINE implementation that the manuscript does not yet reflect. Nothing here has been applied to `paper.md`. Each note quotes the passage it affects. Sources are in the kaine repository's archived OpenSpec changes (`openspec/changes/archive/`).

## 1. What a research run records (implementation change of 2026-10-03)

Research studies now keep only the global workspace graph. They no longer record every broadcast's content. The source is `2026-10-03-workspace-graph-only-recording`.

- **The graph record.** For each successful broadcast, the ignition log stores:
  - the coalition members (module, event type, salience, original timestamp);
  - the salience scores and the inhibition decision;
  - timing, programme position and time scale.

  It stores no payloads, is written at full rate, and is about 11 MB per hour of running.
- **What studies no longer record.** Studies never enable the Nexus record (every diagnostics event) or the workspace trajectory. The trajectory itself records only the graph: no member payloads and no affect state.
- **The privacy filter strips vectors.** It removes perceptual and latent vectors (vision clip embeddings, the temporal module's hidden state) from every diagnostics surface, so no embedding of what the entity saw is displayed or stored.
- **The lifecycle of the graphs.** They are kept for the analysis, then as training data for the world model, and are deleted by the operator after both uses, never automatically. Nothing reads them for training yet.

Passages to revise:
- **"The evaluation sidecar records workspace data during the research phase, and we acknowledge the tension: recording every broadcast is comprehensive observation of the content the system makes globally available…"** Every broadcast is still recorded, but as a graph, without the content that was broadcast. The tension is much narrower and should be restated.
- **"The sidecar runs independent observers writing daily-rotated JSONL: the workspace trajectory with every broadcast and its salience and precision, …"**
  - The primary record is now the ignition log (the workspace graph).
  - Check the word "precision": the log stores salience scores and inhibition, not a separate precision value.
  - The module-attribution, prediction-error and affect observers still run.
- **"… the comprehensive sidecar recording during the research phase, …"** (risks paragraph). Restate it as graph-only recording.
- **Phantasia: "… trained on accumulated workspace trajectories, so it begins untrained at first boot."** Add the lifecycle above. Phantasia today trains only on an in-memory buffer of waking trajectories that is never saved. Stored graphs are intended as future offline training data.

## 2. Gestation: earned entrainment (implementation change of 2026-10-03)

The manuscript does not describe gestation yet. If a gestation or developmental section is added, for example for the module-ignition study, these points need stating. The source is `2026-10-03-self-rhythm-earned-entrainment` (`design.md`, `validation.md`, `references.bib` with 21 sources).

- **Mechanism.**
  - The self-rhythm is a breathing-like rhythm (0.5–0.9 Hz), generated by excitatory recurrence with synaptic depression (Tabak et al. 2000).
  - The maternal heartbeat enters through a weak, bounded afferent.
  - The rhythm's period adapts slowly through a phase-projected rule (Righetti, Buchli and Ijspeert 2006).
  - Locking is earned over lived exposure and can fail. The beat frequency appears nowhere in the code.
- **The marker.** Entrainment counts when all of these hold:
  - the band-limited phase-locking value beats all 19 "foreign mother" surrogate beats (p < 0.05; Van Leeuwen et al. 2003, 2009);
  - the rhythm self-sustains when the beat is withdrawn;
  - its frequency stays pulled toward the mother's rate;
  - the result replicates over three consecutive withdrawals.

  There is no fixed PLV floor, and no published study reports sustained 1:1 PLV ≥ 0.5.
- **Validation (offline, 96 h).**
  - At 70 bpm all 10 seeds entrain, typically by about 14 h.
  - Each rate from 60 to 80 bpm tracks its own mother.
  - Every control never passes: no drive, jittered beat, no plasticity, and foreign mother.
- **Limitations the paper must disclose** (from `validation.md`):
  1. Timescale compression. The literature reports effects after weeks of exposure (Feldman and Eidelman 2003; Webb et al. 2015). Here the plasticity rate is set so a lock forms within a 24–96 h gestation.
  2. Time to entrainment depends on the mother's rate: about 3 h at 60 bpm, 14 h at 70, and 40–48 h at 80.
  3. Mothers below about 60 bpm cannot demonstrate entrainment, because frequency pull is inconclusive near the rhythm's natural rate.
  4. The model's rhythm runs continuously, whereas fetal breathing is episodic (about 14% of the time; Natale et al. 1988).
  5. 1:1 locking is a modelling choice. Breathing–heart coupling in the literature is weak and often n:m.
  6. The replication count was chosen on the main run's data, then confirmed on fresh seeds and fresh surrogate pairs.
  7. Time dilation (time scale ≠ 1) was not tested with the oscillator.
- **The viability watch.** A gestation is stopped early as unviable if:
  - R0: after 6 h of lived time, no withdrawal has produced a conclusive measurement;
  - R1: at 24 h, frequency pull stays flat and low;
  - R2: at 48 h, frequency pull is too slow;
  - R3: at 60 h, there is still no replicated pass.

  Lived time excludes sleep and freezes, which is the basis the rules were validated on. Scored against all 55 offline conditions, the rules flag no viable gestation. The source is `2026-10-03-gestation-viability-watch`.

## 4. Individuation instrument (implementation change of 2026-10-03)

This manuscript defers the individuation boundary to a separate paper (line 358). That paper, and any sentence here that touches individuation, should describe the rebuilt instrument, not the earlier operator-run fork-versus-parent cosine test, which is retired. The source is the KAINE change `individuation-rebuild` (`design.md`, `validation.md`, `references.bib`).

- **What is compared.** The being's current answers to a fixed battery of 12 preference prompts are compared with its own birth reference: 16 answers per prompt, captured at the birth transition. The current sample is 8 answers per prompt. The probe runs through the being's own language organ, with its own adapter and self-model and with empty working memory. It never enters the being's experience, and the being is told, as a fact about its situation, that it is assessed for its own protection.
- **The test.**
  - It is a stratified two-sample energy-distance permutation test on semantic embeddings, with p = (b+1)/(B+1) and an effect size H.
  - A look runs only when the being's conditioning (voice adapter or identity clause) has changed.
  - The lifetime false-positive rate is bounded at α_total = 0.05 by alpha spending across looks: α_k = α_total / (2.1097 · (k+1) · ln²(k+1)).
  - A significant look latches the being as individuated permanently.
- **Validation (offline simulation, pre-registered).**
  - The test's size is 0.0446 at α = 0.05 and 0.0078 at α = 0.01 (5,000 null datasets).
  - Over 2,000 simulated lives of 100 looks each, the lifetime false-positive rate is 0.038, against 0.903 for testing at p ≤ 0.05 on every look.
  - Pre-registered power at look 10 is 0.990 against cluster drift in all 12 prompts. That is an upper bound: shift drift is the harder model, and the real-organ smoke test is the real power check.
- **One verdict.** Preservation, decommission and the fork merge gate share one decision.
  - A being counts as diverged when it is latched as individuated, its consolidation divergence crosses threshold, its self-model records identity drift, or it carries a trained voice adapter.
  - A non-significant individuation result never suppresses the other signals.
  - Unreadable evidence counts as individuated.
- **Limitations to disclose.**
  1. The effect floor is not yet calibrated. An operator-run real-organ smoke test sets it, and until then it is 0.
  2. Beings born before the change carry a later "capture" reference. Drift before that date is not measured.
  3. Forks cannot yet be measured against a fork-point reference, so a fork that has lived at least 30 minutes is preserved by default.
  4. In adapter hot-swap modes that cannot confirm which adapter the organ serves, probes are skipped once an adapter exists. Such beings are protected by the adapter signal instead.
