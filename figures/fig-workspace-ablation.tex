\node[mod, text width=160mm, align=left, inner sep=3mm] (meas) at (7.5,-6.6)
  {\textbf{Planned measures.}\;\;
   \textbf{Primary}: cross-module broadcast information gain, $\mathrm{gain}_j=(\nu_j(b^0_j)-\nu_j(b))/\bar\nu_j$, the reduction in processor $j$'s prediction error when other modules' share of its context is replaced by its mean so far in the run; averaged over $j$'s reports to $\mathrm{IG}_j$ (Topos, Audition, Soma) and over the three processors to the headline $\mathrm{IG}$, on minus each control; the language organ's share of the context is excluded from Soma's gain.\;
   \textbf{Positive control}: the next input projected on its first principal component, with noise set from the pilot runs so that the expected effect equals the minimum effect $\theta_{\rm PC}$; it must be detected for every processor before the live runs.\;
   \textbf{Secondary}: the contrast at threshold, a local-linear regression discontinuity at the access threshold with robust bias-corrected inference, matched on context age; and the tone analysis, which compares the accessed fractions of emotional and neutral tone events in the tone variant, overall and within bins of the error ratio. Neither enters the verdict.\;
   Utterances are recorded but are not a measure.};
\node[halt, text width=160mm, align=left, inner sep=3mm, below=2.5mm of meas] (out)

   \textbf{NOT EXERCISED}, for a run against a control: competition never operates (candidates never exceed the coalition's capacity on a broadcast tick), the on arm has no accessed broadcast, or the run's contrast is undefined. Such runs stay out of the sign tests, and runs are added until 102 enter each control's test; the verdict is NOT EXERCISED only when every run is.};
